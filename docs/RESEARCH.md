# Research and product hypothesis

Researched 2026-10-05. [Full method, alternative explanations and evidence ledger](COMMUNITY-RESEARCH-2026-10-05.md).

## Why this workflow

Later Lane focuses on returning to a small reading intention. R4 shows why organizing alone may be insufficient; R5 supplies a reason to keep user-controlled links and backups. The app does not recover platform bookmarks.

## What we built from the observation

Every item needs a user-written purpose and duration estimate. The tray optimizes explicit priority plus bounded waiting age under a time budget, with a three-card limit. A passed reconsideration date separates a decision from a deletion. Reading happens on the linked original page.

## Selected primary observations

- [R4 · Search index: 2026-08-22](https://www.reddit.com/r/BookmarkManagers/comments/1vv75ls/how_do_you_deal_with_saving_too_many_articles_and/): The author saves across several places but rarely returns. Some replies report that better storage alone has not changed revisiting; many replies promote their own tools.
- [R5 · Search index: late September 2026; exact publication metadata unavailable](https://www.reddit.com/r/Twitter/comments/1wpz9k5/bookmarks_only_loading_to_a_certain_point_then/): The author reports trouble retrieving older saved posts. It motivates portable personal links, but is not proof of a platform-wide bug or a bug this app can repair.

## Next user session

Ask someone to choose a 15-minute tray from their own five links, complete one reading and write a takeaway. Compare with their usual bookmark workflow without assuming that less stored content is better.

No participant study, adoption numbers or measured productivity claims are attached to this release. Posts are a small nonrandom sample; promotional posts, cached dates and partial X text are identified in the full ledger. Example app data is fictional and is not a copy of the sources.
